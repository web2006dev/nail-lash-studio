Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do I need an advance appointment, or do you take walk-ins?

Walk-ins are warmly welcomed subject to technician availability! However, to avoid waiting times—especially during weekends, festivals, and bridal season—we strongly recommend pre-booking via our WhatsApp or phone at 084275 20007.

Q: How long do Gel & Acrylic Nail Extensions last?

Our high-grade acrylic and gel sculpts typically last 3 to 5 weeks depending on natural nail growth rate. We recommend a quick refill at 3–4 weeks to rebalance the apex and maintain optimal nail health.

Q: Is Microblading / PMU painful?

Client comfort is our foremost priority. We apply premium topical anesthetic numbing cream 25 minutes prior to and continuously during the procedure. Most clients report feeling only a light scratching sensation or even fall asleep during the session!

Q: Can I visit the studio for an Academy orientation before enrolling?

Absolutely! We encourage prospective students and parents to visit our Phase 10 facility, observe ongoing live model batches, review our starter kits, and speak directly with our head educator.
